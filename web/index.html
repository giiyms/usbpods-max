USBPods Max

AirPods control for the Pico 2 W dongle — Chrome or Edge, no driver.

If the Mac or PC’s own Bluetooth is connected to the AirPods, A2DP on this dongle dies and speakers go silent. Disconnect the headset from the host Bluetooth stack first. USB-C on AirPods Max 2 is playback-only — this dongle is the 2-way path.

Digital gain on the Pico after AAC-ELD decode (0 to +24 dB, default 0). Soft-clip limiter; not HFP. Same control as Windows’ recording slider (UAC Feature Unit) and the serial gain command.

A/B mic clip

Noise control

AACP control 0x09 / 0x0D from librepods. Max 2 has all four modes.

Max 2 host grab

Handshake replays LibrePods control set: own=1, Connect Automatically 0x20=0x01 (keeps this host). Allow Auto Connect 0x36 is not sent (Android: 0x20 is the one used). iPhone may still need “Connect only When Last Connected”.

auto-conn — 0x36 not sent ear-detect — gestures —

Crown, clicks, listening

Raw gestures 0x0F, listen mask 0x0F (Off|ANC|Trans|Adaptive), click/hold mapped on the host from 0x0019 (LibrePods StemAction defaults). 0x14/0x15 are not sent — no packet values in LibrePods.

Pairing

Pair = long-press BOOTSEL on the Pico, then put the case in pairing mode. Host Bluetooth must not own the headset.

Flash firmware

Hold the Pico’s BOOTSEL button, unplug, plug back in, let go. Copy the UF2 onto the RP2350 drive. That’s how you switch at work too. Chrome/Edge, no driver.
